# Daily Standup

<summary>
Meet daily to organize work, facilitate learning, and improve your productivity and effectiveness.
</summary>

A Daily Standup is [time-boxed](glossary:time-box) (usually 15 minutes) and held daily at the same time.

The team gathers around a visible project management board/tool to:

-   organize daily work
-   identify and resolve impediments/blocks
    -   adapt existing [policies](glossary:policy) or create new ones on the fly
