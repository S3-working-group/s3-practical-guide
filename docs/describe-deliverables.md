<div class="card summary"><div class="card-body">Clearly describe any deliverables related to a policy to support shared understanding of expectations.
</div></div>

_A **deliverable** is a product or service provided in the context of an intervention. Products include components and materials._

When describing deliverables:

-   include the necessary amount of detail
-   reference other documents when helpful or necessary

Explicitly describing deliverables can be useful for improving communication and collaboration within the organization, with customer and with external partners.
